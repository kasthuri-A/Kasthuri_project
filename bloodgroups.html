Blood Groups

Learn about the different blood groups and their basic donation compatibility.

A+

A Positive

Can donate red cells to A+ and AB+ recipients.

A-

A Negative

Can donate red cells to A and AB blood groups.

B+

B Positive

Can donate red cells to B+ and AB+ recipients.

B-

B Negative

Can donate red cells to B and AB blood groups.

AB+

AB Positive

Can donate red cells to AB+ recipients.

AB-

AB Negative

Can donate red cells to AB- and AB+ recipients.

O+

O Positive

Can donate red cells to positive blood groups.

O-

O Negative

O negative red cells can be used for emergency transfusions when the blood type is unknown.

Why Blood Groups Matter?

Knowing the correct blood group is important before blood transfusion. Hospitals perform proper blood grouping and compatibility testing before transfusion.
